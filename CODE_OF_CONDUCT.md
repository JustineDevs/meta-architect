# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to make participation
welcoming and technically constructive. Be respectful, assume good faith, use
precise language, and focus criticism on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information
without consent.

## Maintainer action

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or
restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Decisions should be
proportionate, documented, and applied consistently.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact `justinedevs@jstn.site`. For security issues,
follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) instead of filing a public issue.
